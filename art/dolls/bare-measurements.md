# HearthHold bare-body measurements (skin, 2026-11-09)

120x200 canvas, pixels. Measured from the new bare bodies in `art/dolls/bare/`, wearing tight underclothes only. Human, Elf, Crocasian and Mau'kai share the human frame below the neck; Dwarves have their own stockier frame. Chest, waist and hip are torso widths without arms; shoulders include the arms. Thigh, knee and ankle are one leg's width. Knee y is estimated (45% of crotch-to-foot) because the knee has no clear notch in the outline. Ears, horns and the Mau'kai tail are not counted in widths.
